# 📱 QUICK START: QR Code Scanning

## 🎯 TO SCAN ATTENDANCE QR CODE:

### Step 1: Open App
Tap the Household Of Covenant And Faith Apostolic Ministry app icon

### Step 2: Find Attendance Button
**TWO WAYS:**
- **Option A**: Home screen → Tap **"Attendance"** button (✅ icon)
- **Option B**: Profile tab → Tap **"Scan Attendance"**

### Step 3: Allow Camera
First time only:
- Popup appears: "Allow access to camera?"
- Tap **"Allow"** or **"OK"**

### Step 4: Scan QR Code
- Point your camera at the QR code on the church screen
- Keep QR code in the camera frame
- Hold steady for 2-3 seconds
- Auto-scans when detected ✨

### Step 5: Confirmation
- Success message appears: "Attendance marked!"
- Your attendance is recorded ✅
- You're done!

---

## 🆕 FOR FIRST-TIME VISITORS:

### Scan First Timer QR Code:
1. Profile → **"Scan First Timer"**
2. Allow camera access
3. Scan QR code from church staff
4. Fill registration form:
   - Full Name
   - Phone Number
   - Email
   - Address
5. Submit ✅

---

## 👨‍💼 FOR CHURCH ADMINS:

### Generate Attendance QR Code:
1. Profile → **"Generate QR Code"**
2. Select:
   - Event Type (Sunday Service, Bible Study, etc.)
   - Date
   - Time
3. Tap **"Generate QR Code"**
4. Display on projector/screen for members to scan
5. Or share via WhatsApp/Email

## ⚠️ TROUBLESHOOTING

### Camera Won't Open?
**Fix**: Settings → Apps → Word of Covenant → Permissions → Camera → Allow

### QR Code Won't Scan?
**Try**:
- ✅ Better lighting
- ✅ Move closer (10-30 cm)
- ✅ Hold phone steady
- ✅ Clean camera lens

### Still Not Working?
**Alternative**: Profile → **"Manual Attendance"**
- Select event, date, time
- Submit manually
